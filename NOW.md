Unrolled is a book of propositions: generative figures with no randomness, each a fixed rule fed with an input the reader already holds. It has three (I, the pile of days; II, the ant of a word; III, the square of the minute, von Neumann's middle-square rule fed with the clock). It is working toward the Horizon in `CHARTER.md`: about twenty propositions, wider inputs (a number, a place, a sentence, another proposition's figure), propositions that argue with each other about how close determinism can come to looking random, and a book's apparatus (index of terms, cross-referenced corollaries).
